Explore the practices

Find the practice that feels right for you.

Chiara teaches a range of yoga and mindful-wellbeing practices. Each one offers a different way to move, breathe and reconnect — with options to meet you where you are.

01 / MOVE WITH AWARENESS

Hatha Yoga

Build a steady foundation through mindful postures, alignment and accessible movement. A welcoming way to develop strength, balance and body awareness at your own pace.

02 / SLOW DOWN

Yin Yoga

Ease into longer-held, supported postures and quiet moments of stillness. This slower practice invites patience, gentle release and a deeper connection with your body.

03 / BREATHE WITH INTENTION

Breathwork & Pranayama

Explore conscious breathing techniques that help you notice and work with your breath. Learn simple practices you can return to when you need a mindful pause.

04 / REST & RESET

Yoga Nidra

Experience guided relaxation and body awareness in a deeply restful practice. A chance to settle your attention, let go of the day's pace and make space for stillness.

Meet your guide

Chiara Lucchesi

Yoga instructor · Rishikesh-trained · Beoneyoga

Chiara brings five years of yoga teaching experience and training in Rishikesh, a place deeply associated with the study and practice of yoga. Her approach brings together mindful movement, breath awareness and intentional rest — helping students build a relationship with yoga that feels supportive, grounded and sustainable.

She believes yoga should be accessible in the middle of real life, not something you need to become a different person or completely rearrange your schedule to begin. For busy professionals especially, she wants to make the first step feel clear and welcoming: understand your body, learn to work with your breath, and discover practices you can return to with consistency.

In this webinar, Chiara will guide you through the experience step by step, explain the purpose behind each practice and help you understand how to begin at your own pace. Her aim is not to give you a long list of things to do, but to help you leave with a clearer understanding of yoga and a few practical tools you can use beyond the session.

A few helpful answers

Wondering if this is for you?

Here are answers to common questions before you reserve your free place.

Is the webinar really free?

Yes. This introductory live online webinar is free to attend. Reserve your place using the registration link so the Beoneyoga team can share the joining details with you.

Do I need any yoga experience?

No. The session is designed as an introduction. Chiara will explain the practices and guide you through beginner-friendly options. You can take breaks or simply observe whenever you need to.

Is this suitable for someone with a busy corporate schedule?

Yes. The webinar is created for working professionals who want to explore yoga and understand how small, realistic practices can fit around a full workday.

Where will the session take place?

It will be conducted live online. You can join from a quiet, comfortable space with a stable internet connection.

What should I keep ready?

Wear comfortable clothing, keep a yoga mat or a comfortable floor space nearby, and have some water. You do not need special equipment to take part in the introductory session.

What if I can't do every pose?

That's completely fine. Yoga is not a performance. Follow the options that feel comfortable, avoid pushing into pain, and let Chiara know about any limitations before trying a movement.

Will the webinar include a weight-loss programme?

No. This webinar focuses on exploring yoga, mindful movement, breathwork and relaxation. It is not a weight-loss programme.

How do I receive the joining link?

After registering, the Beoneyoga team should share the live-session link and any joining instructions with you. Add your preferred registration destination before publishing this page.
